When does the queue drain?

A queue holds requests waiting for service. Choose whole requests per second. Run one minute of peak traffic followed by one minute of recovery. A queue limit of zero drops all work beyond the current second's capacity.

Check your reasoning

At 120 arrivals and 100 completions per second, the backlog grows by 20 each second. In 60 seconds that makes 1,200. Recovery at 80 arrivals frees 20 slots per second. Raise recovery arrivals to 100 and see why returning traffic to capacity leaves the backlog in place.
