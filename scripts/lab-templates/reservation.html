At 120 arrivals and 100 completions per second, the backlog grows by 20 each second. In 60 seconds that makes 1,200. Recovery at 80 arrivals frees 20 slots per second. Raise recovery arrivals to 100 and see why returning traffic to capacity leaves the backlog in place.

What evidence distinguishes loss from duplication?

The worker reconnects, reads the pending event and retries it. Exactly one grant should have exactly one receipt. A separately committed processed ID can suppress the missing receipt. A missing identity check lets a committed receipt repeat. All writes here use IndexedDB. Stopping between transactions and aborting an in-flight transaction model different crash boundaries. An external email provider would need its own delivery contract.
