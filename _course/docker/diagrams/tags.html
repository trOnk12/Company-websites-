1. A repository, notes, holds images of one program. Each image is known by its digest: this one, 6b8731de…, has a notes.txt that says hello.

  2. An image tag is a short, human name pointing at one image: v1, and also stable. Two tags, one image.

  3. A new version, 640e63e0…, says hello, world. Tags v2 and latest point at it. latest is only a name: nothing makes it newest.

  4. Someone runs docker tag notes:v2 notes:stable and pushes. Predict: what does pulling notes:stable give now, and what does pulling @sha256:6b8731de… give?

  5. The tag stable moved: it now points at 640e63e0…. Nothing else changed: both images are still there, under their digests.

  6. So notes:stable now gives hello, world, while notes@sha256:6b8731de… gives hello, today and forever.
