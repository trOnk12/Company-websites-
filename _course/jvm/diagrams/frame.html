1. Calling increment(original) creates a new stack frame, the memory for one call. 7 is copied from main's local original into increment's slot 0.

  2. iload_0 copies the 7 in slot 0 onto increment's operand stack, the pile its instructions work on.

  3. iconst_1 pushes 1 on top. Predict: what does iadd leave on the stack?

  4. One value, 8: iadd took both 7 and 1 off the top and pushed their sum.

  5. ireturn moves 8 onto main's stack, and increment's frame is thrown away. main's original still holds 7.
