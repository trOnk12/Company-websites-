1. This app uses an Activity to host a screen. One Activity can also host several navigation destinations.

  2. Android runs a process of its own, system_server: the town hall. It keeps the register of every Activity of every app.

  3. It moves each Activity along a path of stages, the lifecycle: created (set up), started (visible), resumed. Resumed means in front and ready for taps. Predict: what happens when the user switches to another app?

  4. system_server sends your app a message each time (puzzle 5 shows how): your Activity moves to paused (no longer in front), then stopped (hidden). Your app doesn't choose this; it's told.

  5. When the screen is closed for good, system_server moves it to destroyed.
