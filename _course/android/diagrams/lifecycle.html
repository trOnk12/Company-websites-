1. Each screen of your app is an Activity. Your app shows one now.

  2. Android runs a process of its own, system_server: the town hall. It keeps the register of every Activity of every app.

  3. It moves each Activity along a path of stages, the lifecycle: created, started, resumed. Resumed means on screen and ready for taps. Predict: what happens when the user switches to another app?

  4. system_server moves your Activity to paused, then stopped. Your app doesn't choose this; it's told.

  5. When the screen is closed for good, system_server moves it to destroyed.
