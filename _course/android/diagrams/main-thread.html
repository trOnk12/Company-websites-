1. Inside your app, the main thread works through a queue of letters: draw a frame, run your onCreate, handle a touch. One at a time, in order.

  2. Remember the couriers from puzzle 5? When the town hall says "resume", a Binder thread receives it and drops it at the end of the queue with a Handler, the slot into this queue.

  3. The clerk takes the first letter, finishes it, and the queue moves up. Predict: what if the next letter takes a long time?

  4. Your onCreate calls Thread.sleep(6000): it blocks for 6 seconds. Everything behind it waits, including the user's touch: the clerk only does one thing at a time.

  5. If a touch isn't handled within 5 seconds, the system shows App isn't responding (an ANR). Long before that, the screen stops being redrawn: the UI freezes.
