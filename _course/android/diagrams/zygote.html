1. At boot, Android starts one process, Zygote, and loads ART and the framework into its memory once. Then it waits.

  2. You tap an icon. Zygote forks: the kernel makes a new process that starts as a copy of Zygote, switches it to your app's UID, and loads your code.

  3. The new process doesn't get its own copy of the framework: it shares Zygote's memory pages. So does every other app. Predict: what if your app changes a shared page?

  4. Only then does the kernel copy that one page for your app: copy-on-write. Everything you don't change stays shared.
