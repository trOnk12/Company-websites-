1. When the phone starts up, at boot, Android starts one process, Zygote. It starts ART and loads the framework, Android's own code that every app uses. Then it waits.

  2. You open an app. Zygote asks the kernel to fork: make a new process that starts as a copy of Zygote. Predict: what's already loaded in the copy?

  3. Everything: ART and the framework. The copy switches to your app's UID and loads your app's code. No waiting.

  4. Zygote did the same at boot for system_server: the town hall started as a copy too.
