1. On the phone, an app you've installed is just files. Tap the Notes icon, and the phone starts it running: Notes is now a process.

  2. Open Music too, and you have two processes, side by side.

  3. Each process keeps its data while it runs, like the note you're typing, in its own memory. Predict: what happens to your note if Music crashes?

  4. Nothing. Music's process ends, and Notes keeps running with its memory untouched. Processes are kept apart.
