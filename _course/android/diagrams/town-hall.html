1. Every app lives in its own sandbox. So who decides which app is on screen, which app opens a link, which app gets killed when memory runs low? One trusted process that sees everything: system_server, the town hall.

  2. Your app calls startActivity() to open its own next screen. Even so, the request leaves your process and goes to the town hall's ActivityTaskManager.

  3. The town hall checks the request (does this screen exist? may you open it?) and writes it in its register: it now knows your app has screens A and B, in that order.

  4. Only then does it tell your app: "create B". Your app builds Screen B because it was told to. Your screens' lives are recorded and decided outside your app.
