1. Your app shows screen A. The town hall, system_server, keeps the official register of every app's screens. Right now it says: your app, A.

  2. Your app calls startActivity() to open its own screen B. Even so, the request leaves your house and goes to the town hall (by Binder, the post office from puzzle 5).

  3. The town hall checks it: does B exist, may you open it? Then it writes B in the register. Predict: has screen B been created yet?

  4. Not until now: the town hall sends back "create B", and only then does your app build the screen. Your screens' lives are decided outside your app.
