1. The town hall, system_server, and your app are two separate processes, both standing on the kernel.

  2. The town hall writes a message: "resume your screen". But it can't reach into your app's memory. Predict: who can?

  3. Only the kernel reaches every process. It's built from parts that each do one job, called a driver; the one that carries messages is Binder: the post office. It carries them both ways.

  4. Binder delivers the message into your app. Every message between your app and the town hall travels this way.
