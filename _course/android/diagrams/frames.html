1. The screen is redrawn 60 times a second; each redraw is a frame, due about every 16 milliseconds (ms). Each frame is a message in the main thread's queue.

  2. One message takes too long. The frames behind it can't be drawn in time, and the app stutters: jank.

  3. The user taps. system_server sends the touch, and it waits in the queue too. Predict: what if it waits much longer?

  4. system_server waits for your app to say the touch is handled. After 5 seconds with no answer, it shows "App isn't responding": an ANR (Application Not Responding).
