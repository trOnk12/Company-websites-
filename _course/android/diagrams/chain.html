1. Your app's process, with the main thread at its desk. On the desk: your onCreate(), the method that sets up a screen, waiting.

  2. The town hall, system_server, decides: "create screen X". It writes the message.

  3. Binder, the post office in the kernel, carries it. Predict: where does it land?

  4. On the main thread's desk. The main thread handles one message at a time, in order.

  5. The main thread runs your code: onCreate(). You wrote it; you never called it. It's a callback.
