1. Your app is a house with one clerk inside: the main thread. Your code waits on the desk. Notice what's missing: nothing in the house decides when your screen starts.

  2. That decision is made in another process, system_server: the town hall. It writes the order "start screen X". Predict: can the town hall just walk into your house?

  3. No: processes can't touch each other's memory. The letter goes through Binder, a driver in the kernel: the post office, the only one allowed into every house.

  4. The letter lands in the clerk's in-tray. The clerk handles letters one at a time, in order. (A small helper actually carries it in; you'll meet it in puzzle 6.) Predict: what happens next?

  5. The clerk runs your code: onCreate(). You didn't call it. You were called. Every puzzle in this course is one piece of this chain.
