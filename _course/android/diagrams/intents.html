1. The chat app wants a photo. It can't reach into the camera app: they're in separate sandboxes. So it writes an Intent, a request to open the camera's screen.

  2. The Intent goes to the town hall, system_server, by Binder, like every message to it.

  3. The town hall checks its list: has Chat been granted the camera permission, the right to use the camera? Predict: what if it hadn't?

  4. Chat is allowed, so the town hall delivers the request, and the camera screen opens.

  5. A game without the permission asks the same: the town hall refuses.
