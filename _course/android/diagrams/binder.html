1. Your app wants the town hall to start a screen, but the town hall lives in another process. In your house sits a proxy: a counter that looks exactly like the town hall's service.

  2. Objects can't leave a process; bytes can. The proxy packs the call's arguments into a flat envelope: a Parcel.

  3. The kernel's Binder driver carries it, and stamps it with your app's UID. The receiver can't be fooled about who sent it. Predict: who picks it up at the town hall?

  4. The town hall keeps a few couriers waiting for mail: its Binder threads. One takes the Parcel, unpacks it, and calls the real method.

  5. The reply travels back the same way, and the proxy call in your app returns.

  6. Each process's mailbox for incoming calls holds about 1 MB, shared by all calls in flight. A 3 MB bitmap in an Intent doesn't fit: TransactionTooLargeException.
