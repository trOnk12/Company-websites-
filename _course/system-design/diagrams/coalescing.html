1. The first west caller creates a pending entry and starts one storage read.

  2. The second west caller arrives before the result. Follow its arrow to the existing west entry. No second arrow goes to storage.

  3. East needs different data. It creates a separate entry and starts another storage read.

  4. Three callers receive results from two underlying reads. On completion each pending entry is removed. If west fails, both west callers share that failure.
