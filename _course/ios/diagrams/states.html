1. UIKit keeps your app in one of a few app states: from not running to active, in front and taking touches, where your code runs freely; the last is suspended.

  2. The user leaves: your app moves to the background, with a few seconds, five at most, to finish crucial work.

  3. Then iOS suspends it: still in memory, but no code runs, on any thread. The upload stops. Predict: what if memory is needed?

  4. iOS ends the app, and no code of yours runs at that moment. Save as you enter the background, not at the end.
