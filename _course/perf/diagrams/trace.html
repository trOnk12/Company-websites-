1. A trace: the kernel and the app write events, each with a timestamp, into one recording. Afterwards the timeline can be rebuilt.

  2. Perfetto draws it: time runs left to right, and each thread gets a row, a track.

  3. On a track, each slice is a named span with a start and an end. Predict: what does a slice drawn under another one mean?

  4. It happened inside it: measure() and draw() ran as part of that frame's work.
