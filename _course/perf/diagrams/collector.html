1. Each thread allocates from its own TLAB: a new object moves a pointer forward. No lock, no search.

  2. Concurrent Copying copies the objects still alive into fresh space while your threads keep running. One small pause per collection: a moment when your threads stop.

  3. Most objects die young, so a young GC looks only at new ones: cheap and frequent. Predict: when does this still cost a frame?

  4. With allocation churn: allocate faster than the collector frees, and your main thread must wait for it.
