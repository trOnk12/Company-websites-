1. Perfetto's FrameTimeline draws two slices per frame. First the Expected Timeline: each frame's window. Its end is the frame's deadline.

  2. The Actual Timeline: when each frame's work really ran. The first two end inside their windows: on time.

  3. The third: the main thread ends early, but the GPU finishes after the deadline. FrameTimeline marks it late, the app's fault. Predict: where next?

  4. Into that frame's main thread and RenderThread: their states first, then the flame chart for the Running parts.
