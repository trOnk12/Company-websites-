1. Your app's RenderThread sends drawing commands to the GPU, the graphics processor.

  2. The GPU writes the frame's pixels into a buffer, memory that holds one picture. Your app queues it.

  3. SurfaceFlinger takes the latest buffer from every visible window and composites them. Predict: what if your buffer isn't finished in time?

  4. At vsync the display shows the combined picture. No finished buffer from your app: its old picture is shown again.
