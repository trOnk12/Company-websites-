1. A frame starts with the first phase, composition: the marked composables run (puzzles 2 and 3), and the tree has three nodes. The screen area on the right is still empty.

  2. Layout begins at the top. The Column gets constraints from its parent: the smallest and largest size it may take, here at most 340 pixels wide.

  3. The Column asks each child to measure itself within those constraints, once each: 200×40 and 340×120. Then it picks its own size. Predict: where does the Card go?

  4. Placement: the Column sets each child's position relative to itself. Text at the top, the Card just below it at y = 40.

  5. Drawing: each node paints itself, in its place, onto the canvas, the surface that takes commands such as drawRect.
