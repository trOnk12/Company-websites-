1. Before: the icon reacts to a tap with a raw touch handler, and the picture turns from outline to solid. A sighted user sees the change.

  2. But nothing described it. VoiceOver reads only accessibility semantics: a label, a role, a state, actions. Here there are none, so VoiceOver has nothing to say.

  3. After: the same icon, now with Modifier.toggleable, Role.Switch and a content description. Predict: what can VoiceOver say now?

  4. Compose's semantics now hold a label, a role and an on/off state, and Compose Multiplatform maps them to the element's iOS accessibility label, traits and value.
