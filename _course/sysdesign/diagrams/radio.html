1. The phone's radio, idle: its radio state is low power, only listening now and then.

  2. A 300-byte ping. The radio wakes to high power, sends it in a blink, then stays awake for its tail time, waiting for more.

  3. Send a ping every 20 seconds, and each tail runs into the next: the radio never sleeps. Predict: what lets it sleep?

  4. Batching: queue the pings and send them together. One wake-up, one tail, and the radio sleeps the rest of the time.
