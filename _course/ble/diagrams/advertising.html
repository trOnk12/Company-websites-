1. The strap wakes and sends a packet, one short burst of data: "I'm here". Then it sleeps again. Doing this over and over is advertising.

  2. It repeats at its advertising interval, at least 20 ms apart, plus a random 0 to 10 ms, so two straps don't keep sending at the same moment and garbling each other.

  3. The airwaves are split into 40 channels. Advertising uses only 3, and each advertisement goes out on all 3. Predict: where does the phone listen?

  4. On those 3, one at a time: it's scanning. When a packet lands on the channel it's listening to, the phone hears the strap.
