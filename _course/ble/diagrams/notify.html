1. To show a live heart rate by reading, the phone asks at every meeting: a request and an answer each time, even when nothing changed.

  2. Instead, the phone flips a switch on the strap: a descriptor, an extra setting attached to the value, called the CCCD. It writes "on".

  3. Now, when the value changes, the strap sends it by itself at the next meeting: a notification. Predict: what if the app only turns notifications on in the phone?

  4. Nothing arrives. setCharacteristicNotification() only prepares your phone; without writeDescriptor() the strap's switch stays off: "connected, but no data".
