1. The phone answers an advertisement: "connect?". Now they're in a connection. The phone is the central, which sets the timing; the strap is the peripheral.

  2. The central sets a timetable: meet every connection interval, from 7.5 ms up to 4 s. In between, both radios sleep.

  3. Each meeting is a connection event: both wake at the agreed moment, exchange packets, and sleep. Predict: is every meeting on the same channel?

  4. No: each event uses a different channel, picked by a rule both sides know. Noise on one channel only spoils the events that land on it.
