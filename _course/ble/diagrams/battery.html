1. A heart strap runs on a coin-sized battery. Its radio, the part that sends and listens over the air, is its hungriest part.

  2. Keep the radio on all the time, even just listening, and the battery is flat in days. Predict: how can it last for months?

  3. Sleep. Bluetooth Low Energy, BLE, keeps the radio off almost all the time and wakes it for short, planned moments.

  4. Which makes a new problem: a phone has to be listening at exactly those moments to hear it. The next puzzles show how they meet.
