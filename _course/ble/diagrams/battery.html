1. A heart strap runs on a coin-sized battery. Its radio, the part that sends and listens over the air, is its hungriest part.

  2. Keep the radio on all the time, even just listening, and the battery is flat in days. Predict: how can it last for months?

  3. Sleep. Bluetooth Low Energy, BLE, keeps the radio off almost all the time and wakes it for a few milliseconds at a time.

  4. Which makes a new problem: a phone can only hear the strap while it's awake. The next two puzzles show how they find each other, and then meet on time.
