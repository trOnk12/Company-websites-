1. TLS, the S in HTTPS, starts with a short exchange: the handshake. Your app says hello and sends its public value (puzzle 2).

  2. The server answers with its public value, its certificate, and a signature over the conversation so far (puzzle 4).

  3. Your app checks all three: a trusted authority, the right name, a valid signature. Predict: what's left before data can flow?

  4. Both compute the shared secret and turn it into symmetric keys. Every request now travels encrypted, with a check that catches any change.
