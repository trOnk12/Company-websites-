1. TLS, the S in HTTPS, starts with a short exchange: the handshake. Your app says hello and sends its public value (puzzle 2).

  2. The server answers with its public value. Both compute the shared secret and derive keys from it: from now on, everything is encrypted.

  3. Now, encrypted, the server sends its certificate and a signature over the conversation; your app checks all three. Predict: what protects the data after that?

  4. Each record of data is encrypted with those keys and carries a keyed check: read, change and pretend are all stopped.
