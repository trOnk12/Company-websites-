1. Your app sends a password. It doesn't fly straight to your server: it passes through the café's Wi-Fi and many machines after it.

  2. Any of them can read it as it passes: every byte, in plain sight.

  3. In fact, any of them could do three things: read it, change it, or pretend to be your server. Predict: what stops the reading?

  4. Encryption: with a secret key, your app turns the data into gibberish, ciphertext. The listener sees bytes that mean nothing.
