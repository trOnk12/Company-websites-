1. Any data, a word or a 10 MB file, goes into a hash function. The standard one is SHA-256.

  2. Out comes a short digest, always 256 bits: a fingerprint of the data. Same data, same digest, every time.

  3. Flip a single bit of the file, and the digest is completely different. Predict: how does that catch a change on the way?

  4. Hash what arrived and compare with the digest you expected. Different: someone changed the data.
