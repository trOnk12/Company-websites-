1. Two binaries: the app's executable, built by Xcode from Swift, and your Kotlin. Swift's calls into Kotlin still need addresses. Same start, two endings.

  2. A static library is joined at build time: Xcode's linker copies your Kotlin into the executable.

  3. One file reaches the phone. Predict: what reaches the phone if your Kotlin isn't copied in?

  4. A dynamic library isn't copied in: two files reach the phone, your Kotlin embedded beside the executable.

  5. At every launch, the dynamic loader, dyld, on the phone, loads the Kotlin file and connects Swift's calls to it.
