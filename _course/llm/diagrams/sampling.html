1. At each step the model scores every possible next token: "Great" likely, "Good" less, "Fine" rarely.

  2. One is chosen by sampling: at random, weighted by the scores. Usually "Great", not always.

  3. Low temperature tilts the odds further toward the top token. Predict: what does a high one do?

  4. It evens them out: "Good" and "Fine" get picked more often, so answers vary more.
